# Generations and Knowledge

Civilization continuity is not a global technology save file.

Each resident carries imperfect knowledge. Knowledge survives when:
- parents/guardians teach children
- apprentices observe skilled residents
- peers communicate
- artifacts preserve usable design information
- institutions later preserve records/training

Knowledge can mutate, become ritualized, be misunderstood or disappear when carriers die.

Biological inheritance and cultural inheritance are separate:
- physical traits may be heritable
- personality has inherited/developmental variation
- knowledge, language, religion, technology and norms are learned/social

Households are resource/social units, not mandatory nuclear-family templates. A household may contain relatives, partners, dependents or unrelated cooperative residents.

Settlement fission should emerge from spatial/resource/social pressure. There is no "found city at population 100" rule.
