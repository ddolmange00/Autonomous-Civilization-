# Warfare and logistics

Fighting is self-organized. Residents who choose Attack near the same
threat form a warband; there is no appointed leader and no scripted
war stages.

- force comes from headcount plus invented weapons: only purpose-built
  Cut/Pierce/Impact designs count, evaluated against canonical material
  truth. Shelter sticks are nearly useless
- nearby defensive structures add armor through their real defense
  capability; walls must actually exist
- morale is memory: repulsion emboldens, losses break. Broken bands
  disband into fleeing individuals instead of dying pointlessly
- formation courage can grow out of Attack institutions: coordination
  learned in peace transfers to war

Logistics is physics: hauling scales with stature and practiced
carrying, and fighting far from home supply drains hunger. Caravans
and thieves haul what ordinary bodies can carry.
