# Settlement divergence

Settlements are detected from where people actually live. They are not unlocked by population thresholds.

Each persistent settlement identity tracks:
- current center and member IDs
- founding/last-seen year
- culture inferred from observed successful/visible actions
- household food/material pools in the local cluster
- breadth of resident knowledge
- average practiced skills

This creates divergence without preset civilization classes.

Example:
- river-edge village repeatedly gathers, binds, observes water and experiments -> watercraft/material knowledge and experimentation norms may accumulate.
- cliff village repeatedly digs, raises, carries and works loose/rock material -> construction/mineral skills may dominate.
- predator-heavy village repeatedly avoids, communicates and assists -> warning/cooperation norms may dominate.

The profile is descriptive analytics, never a switch that forces future choices.
Residents still choose from local affordances using their own traits, needs, memory and knowledge.
