# Seafaring and craft

No hull, no voyage. Water crossings require Float-function designs in
the household repertoire, evaluated against timber truth for real
flotation, propulsion and steering.

- prototypes are drafted while experimenting near deep water with
  material to spend; skilled guesses test closer to truth
- successes enter the household repertoire (capped); failures are
  logged as evidence and may seed later designs
- migration goals across water embark only with sufficient craft;
  without it, travelers sidestep along the shore as before
- wall-like defensive designs are proposed only under real,
  locally perceived monster threat

There is no water-to-raft unlock script anywhere in this path.
