# Trade and diplomacy

Surplus flows toward deficit between nearby settlements. Nothing moves
without a real differential: no scripted trade routes, no assigned
alliances.

- trade transfers food and material household-to-household and builds
  a contact ledger with growing trust
- theft happens when the very hungry stand near a foreign store;
  witnessed theft damages the settlement-level contact ledger
- stance (hostile/wary/neutral/friendly) is derived from ledger
  history, never assigned

Trust is memory of what actually happened, not a personality trait.
