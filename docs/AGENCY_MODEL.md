# Decentralized Emergent Agency

There is no civilization response script such as:
monster -> casualties -> defense research -> wall -> victory.

The world exposes facts, constraints and affordances. Residents independently perceive only part of that world and choose among locally possible actions.

## Individual decision inputs
- needs: food, safety, rest, belonging, status, curiosity, care
- personality: threat sensitivity, aggression, curiosity, empathy, conformity, persistence, risk tolerance, novelty seeking, social trust, planning horizon
- imperfect knowledge and memories
- learned value of prior actions
- relationships and obligations
- cultural norms visible to that resident
- currently reachable physical affordances
- bounded/noisy prediction of outcomes

## Generic action vocabulary
The core may expose primitives such as move, observe, gather, bind, dig, raise, heat, strike, avoid, hide, assist, communicate and experiment. It must not expose scenario answers such as BuildWallAgainstMonster or InventSword.

Complex behavior is composed from primitives and Design Genomes.

Examples that may emerge from the same threat:
- dig/excavate + inhabit -> underground refuge
- raise/support + inhabit -> elevated settlement
- bind/raise + obstruct -> barrier-like structures
- experiment/strike/pierce -> offensive artifact lineage
- move/carry -> migration
- hide/avoid -> temporal or spatial avoidance
- communicate/assist -> collective defense or evacuation
- nothing effective -> collapse/extinction

None of these is a mandatory chain.

## Culture is output, not initial class
A civilization is not assigned DefensiveCulture or AggressiveCulture. Repeated visible/successful/prestigious actions alter local norms. Conforming residents become more likely to repeat them. Norms can split geographically, disappear, or reverse after failures.

A later observer may describe a culture as defensive, maritime, experimental or militant, but these labels are analytics derived from behavior, not simulation switches.

## Free will in game terms
This is bounded autonomous agency, not metaphysical free will. Agents have different internal states and incomplete information, so identical external events can produce different choices. Deterministic seeded noise preserves reproducibility while allowing individual variation.

## Player orders
Player guidance adds a social/goal signal; it does not directly set actions or outcomes. Residents may prioritize, reinterpret, delay or ignore guidance according to trust, conformity, needs, feasibility and competing pressures.
