# Material model direction

The simulation does not store "stone age", "iron age" or "modern material" unlocks.

A material is a measurable property vector. Future expansion groups:
- mechanical: density, hardness, tensile/compressive/shear strength, toughness, modulus, fatigue, creep
- surface: friction, abrasion, porosity, adhesion
- thermal: conductivity, heat capacity, softening/melting/ignition temperatures, expansion
- chemical/environmental: corrosion, oxidation, water absorption, salt resistance, rot/biodegradation
- manufacturing: workability, castability, forgeability, machinability, joinability
- optical/electrical/magnetic properties when civilizations discover relevant phenomena

Raw resources become processed materials. Drying, heating, hammering, quenching, binding, mixing and later unknown processes modify the property vector. Civilizations observe noisy outcomes rather than reading the ground-truth vector.

Mixtures and alloys must be evaluated from composition + process history rather than unlocked by historical name. A civilization may therefore discover useful composites without ever reproducing Earth's canonical sequence.
