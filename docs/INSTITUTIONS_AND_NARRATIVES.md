# Institutions and narratives

Institutions crystallize when several members of one settlement repeat
the same action: the pattern becomes a role that makes coordinated
work (for example construction crews) faster. Unused institutions fade.

Narratives form where disasters strike without confident understanding:
affected residents who never confidently perceived the true cause drift
toward unseen-agent stories (spirits). Shared stories comfort small
fear in gatherings but never change physical truth.

Both are emergent memory, not scripted content.
