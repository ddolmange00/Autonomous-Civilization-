# Scenario acceptance matrix

- Deep water without flotation/propulsion: traversal must fail.
- Cliff without anchoring/climbing capability: traversal must fail.
- No-metal world: civilization must remain viable through alternate materials or fail for causal reasons; never auto-spawn metal tech.
- Apex monster vs early settlement: extinction/migration is valid; miraculous weapon unlock is invalid.
- Repeated floods: surviving cultures may adapt, but adaptation must arise from evidence/design changes.
- Remote disaster: unrelated civilization state must not change unless causal effects reach it.
- Long run: history and entity counts must stay within configured budgets.
- Multi-seed convergence: reject if independent worlds repeatedly converge on the same named/structural technology sequence without equivalent constraints.
